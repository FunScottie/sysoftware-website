## Description

Pickle Me Happy is a pickleball scoring and session tracker built for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Keep your head in the rally. Start a session on Apple Watch, tap individual scores to add points, long press to correct mistakes, or speak a quick score phrase before the next serve. Your iPhone keeps the full record so you can review every game, session, and trend later.

New built-in Forecast helps you know before you play. Add your upcoming pickleball days, recurring sessions, ladders, open play, or tournaments, and Pickle Me Happy matches them with court weather so you can quickly see whether conditions look Ideal, Good, or worth watching.

Built for real play:

- Score from Apple Watch with tap, press, or voice input
- Track games, wins, losses, points for, and points against
- Start and end pickleball workout sessions from the Watch app
- Check a 10-day court forecast with high/low temps and playable verdicts
- Mark scheduled play days so weather lines up with when you actually play
- See upcoming play with time, location, rain risk, temperature, and court-ready status
- Review Today, History, and Insights on iPhone
- Add or edit games manually when you forgot to track a session
- See Apple Health workout stats alongside your match history
- View monthly, year-to-date, and court forecast widgets
- Track win rate, streaks, session time, court miles, heart rate, and more

Voice scoring works best when your iPhone is nearby. Pickle Me Happy Pro unlocks cellular/cloud voice scoring, unlimited history, insights, and Apple Health widgets.

Pickle Me Happy is designed for casual play, ladder games, club nights, and anyone who wants a clean record of how they played without carrying a phone on court.

Health and location permissions are optional and only used to power the workout, history, insight, forecast, and widget features you enable.

## App Review Notes

Pickle Me Happy has an iPhone app, Apple Watch companion app, WidgetKit extension, HealthKit integration, speech/microphone score input, and StoreKit 2 subscriptions.

No account or login is required. Health access is optional. Location access is optional and used for Forecast/weather features if the tester chooses current location; a saved forecast location can be used instead. Apple Watch scoring can be tested from the watch companion app. Forecast can be tested from the iPhone Forecast tab by adding scheduled play days with the + button. Pro can be tested with the StoreKit subscription products once they are configured in App Store Connect.
