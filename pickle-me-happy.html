FAQ
The quick trust questions.

Do I need Apple Watch?

No. The iPhone app keeps your sessions, games, history, and insights. Apple Watch makes live scoring and workout tracking faster on court.

Does it use Apple Health?

Only with your permission. Health data can power workout context such as time, steps, calories, heart rate, and distance.

Is my score data shared?

Pickle Me Happy is built around personal tracking. It does not sell your information or use third-party advertising trackers.

Privacy

Your rallies are yours.

Pickle Me Happy stores pickleball sessions, game scores, history, settings, widget summaries, and optional Health-related workout data to provide app features. Microphone or speech features are used only when you choose to record scores by voice.
