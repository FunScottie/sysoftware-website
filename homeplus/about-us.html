Our story

Built on service,
not scale.

HomePlus Corporation opened its doors in San Diego in 1999 with a simple mission: provide our customers with the highest level of customer service and a competitive mortgage rate and term. More than 5,000 five-star reviews later, that mission still guides the company today.

We believe borrowers deserve to understand their options — not just click an “apply” button. Every loan begins with a conversation about your goals and continues with clear guidance through the mortgage process.
