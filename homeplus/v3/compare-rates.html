What to compare

A mortgage quote is more than one rate.

HomePlus helps you review the complete structure so an attractive number does not hide a poor fit elsewhere.

01

Interest rate

The note rate used to calculate principal and interest.

02

APR

A broader cost measure that incorporates certain finance charges.

03

Points & lender fees

Upfront costs may change the rate and overall break-even period.

04

Cash to close

Down payment, closing costs, credits, and prepaid items all matter.

05

Monthly payment

Review principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA, and mortgage insurance when applicable.
