Biography

As a Mortgage Loan Originator at Home Plus Mortgage, Melissa is dedicated to providing elite customer service and unwavering advocacy for every client. Transitioning from a distinguished 20-year career as a Doctor of Physical Therapy, she now applies her decades of professional experience to the residential lending landscape. Her mission is to ensure every borrower feels empowered and informed, whether they are navigating their first home purchase or strategically refinancing an existing property. Melissa takes pride in specializing in VA loans, where she honors our veterans by helping them maximize the homeownership benefits, they have earned.

Her meticulous attention to detail and commitment to personalized guidance make her a trusted partner for any borrower. From first-time buyers to seasoned homeowners, she delivers a seamless, service-oriented experience tailored to each unique financial goal. A proud resident of Encinitas, Melissa fully embraces the lifestyle of one of California’s premier beach towns. When she isn't helping clients secure the perfect loan, she is often found surfing, mountain biking, or gardening. Her deep connection to the local community and her appreciation for both the coast and the mountains reflect the balanced, focused approach she brings to every professional partnership.
