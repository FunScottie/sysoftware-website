Biography

Naomi currently resides in Los Angeles, Ca and has been in the mortgage industry for 32 years. She specializes in helping clients with Film, TV and Music Industry related employment, First Time Homebuyers, Reverse Mortgages products as well as FHA, VA and Conventional loans.

Having worked as well in the Entertainment industry gives her an understanding of how people working in the Entertainment Industry's income are structured and can help navigate them through the loan process. Coming from a background as a former Pan Am flight attendant, Naomi enjoys helping others through the loan process when extra help is needed and serving people's needs.
