Biography

Leah Green brings over 20 years of mortgage expertise to the HomePlus team. A graduate of Texas A&M University with a degree in Industrial Distribution, Leah combines her technical background with specialized certifications in loan origination and mortgage planning. Her meticulous approach ensures that every detail of the lending process is handled with precision, providing a seamless experience for every borrower.

Whether you are purchasing your first home, upgrading, or refinancing to leverage your equity, Leah offers a comprehensive financial perspective. She goes beyond interest rates, helping you analyze budget, income, and savings to find a loan structure that truly fits your long-term goals. With Leah’s guidance, the complexities of home finance become a clear, manageable path toward homeownership.
