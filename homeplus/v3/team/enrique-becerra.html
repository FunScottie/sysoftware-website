Biography

Most loan officers will tell you they can do everything. Then your file sits in underwriting for three weeks and nobody picks up the phone. If you’re a real estate investor, a self-employed professional, or anyone whose financial life doesn’t fit neatly into a W-2 box — you already know that finding the right loan officer is harder than it should be. Someone who actually understands your scenario. Who knows the lenders. Who does the work before your deal is at risk. That’s the problem Enrique solves. Before entering mortgage, he spent twenty years as a personal trainer — a career that taught him how to identify what someone actually needs, troubleshoot under pressure, and deliver without excuses. His deepest expertise is in DSCR loans — single family and multifamily five units and above. He knows the lenders, the guidelines, and how to structure deals positioned to close. For self-employed borrowers, bank statement loans are a specialty. And for every other scenario — conventional, FHA, VA, non-QM, second liens — the same obsessive preparation applies.

Before your file ever touches an underwriter, the work is already done. Right loan, right lender, right terms. That’s not a marketing line — that’s just how he works. Six years in mortgage. Licensed in California, Arizona, Florida, Texas, Utah, Oregon, and Tennessee. Colorado coming soon. DSCR loans available nationally. Clients come back. They send their network. That’s the whole model. Have a scenario? Let’s talk.
