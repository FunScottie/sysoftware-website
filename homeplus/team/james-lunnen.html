Biography

James M. Lunnen graduated with a Bachelor’s of Science in Business Finance from California Polytechnic University San Luis Obispo. He then received post graduate certification in Real Estate Development/Finance from the University of California, Irvine. James is a licensed Real Estate Broker and has his license endorsement through the NMLS. He worked under the Deputy Director of the Small Business Administration in Washington DC before becoming a partner at Lunnen Real Estate Development Company.

During this time, Jim put together commercial and residential real estate development deals and arranged financing for these projects. In 2007 he took his knowledge gained in the commercial real estate industry and applied it to residential home loans. In partnership, James Lunnen and Vince Ambrosanio are responsible for funding over 3 billion dollars of residential home loans. This was accomplished through focus on product, personnel and process. Beyond traditional loans, Jim is the managing partner of an investment group that has acquired over 32 million in private money 1st Trust Deeds. Outside of the mortgage industry, Jim has been active in Real Estate Brokerage, Development and Investment.
