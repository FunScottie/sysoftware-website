Biography

Throughout his career, Phil Pizzino has focused on creating a business where customers and employees can thrive. As Founder and CEO of HomePlus Corporation, he has led the company's adoption of technology, marketing, and service practices while keeping the customer foremost. A licensed California Bureau Real Estate Broker and member of the San Diego Association of Realtors, he is a licensed Residential Mortgage Loan Originator by the National Mortgage Licensing System.

Every day at the HomePlus office, Phil and his industry-leading team help customers purchase and refinance homes. They offer the ease of paperless systems, rapid processing, dedicated Residential Mortgage Loan Originators, Realtors, and support staff who are personally invested in each customer’s success.
