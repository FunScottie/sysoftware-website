The loan process

Seven understandable stages.

A HomePlus professional remains your point of contact while the team coordinates documentation, processing, and communication with the parties involved.

  1. 1

    Conversation

    Discuss your property, goals, timing, and financial picture.

  2. 2

    Application

    Provide accurate information through the secure application process.

  3. 3

    Documentation

    Submit requested supporting information through a secure channel.

  4. 4

    Processing

    The loan file is organized, reviewed, and prepared for underwriting.

  5. 5

    Underwriting

    The lender evaluates the application, property, and program requirements.

  6. 6

    Approval & signing

    Final requirements are addressed and closing documents are prepared.

  7. 7

    Funding & closing

    After conditions and signed documents are accepted, funds are disbursed and the transaction closes.
