Tournament director workspace

Change the plan without stopping the game.

Build the structure before cards fly, then adjust current or future levels during play. Track the money, the field, and the decisions that normally end up scattered across notes and calculators.

Stay in the game

Your iPhone becomes the tournament remote.

Leave the iPad on display and control the essentials from your phone. Nearby peer-to-peer connectivity keeps the clock and status synchronized without an account or internet connection.

  • Pause and resume the live clock
  • Adjust time and move between levels
  • Record player eliminations from the table
