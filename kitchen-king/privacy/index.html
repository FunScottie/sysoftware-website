Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Kitchen King is a free entertainment-only game. It does not require an account, does not collect personal data, does not include ads or third-party analytics, and does not offer purchases. Your credits, settings, and gameplay progress stay on your device.

Who we are

Kitchen King is published by SY Software, an independent app studio founded by Scott Yale. For privacy questions, contact support@sysoftware.com.

Information we collect

Kitchen King is designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information. The current version does not collect personal data, use third-party analytics, include third-party advertising trackers, or sell or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

Kitchen King stores gameplay information locally on your device so the game can remember your state and preferences. This may include:

  • Virtual credit balance, current bet, recent game state, and active bonus-round state
  • Royal Rank progress, daily gift and challenge status, jackpot history, and personal records
  • Settings such as sound volume and selected cabinet lighting theme

This information is used only to provide the game experience. SY Software does not operate a gameplay server for Kitchen King.

Free-play credits and gameplay

Kitchen King uses virtual credits only. Credits have no cash value, cannot be purchased, cannot be redeemed, and cannot be transferred. Kitchen King does not include real-money wagering or prizes.

Network access

Kitchen King does not require an internet connection for normal play. The app includes a link to this privacy policy and a support email link, which may open your browser or mail app when selected.

Apple services and diagnostics

Apple may provide crash logs or diagnostics to developers if you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings. Apple may also manage App Store distribution, device backups, and related services according to your Apple account and device settings.

Sharing

SY Software does not sell, rent, or share personal information from Kitchen King. Kitchen King does not include third-party advertising libraries, behavioral tracking SDKs, or in-app purchase services.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay progress and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting Kitchen King generally deletes the app data stored locally with it. Device backups, if enabled, are managed according to your Apple settings.

Children

Kitchen King is a general-audience entertainment game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Kitchen King changes how it handles data. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact
