FAQ
The quick trust questions.

Is this for medical care?

No. PickleStrong is a fitness, warm-up, and recovery app for pickleball players. It is not medical advice.

Does it need Apple Health?

No. You can skip Apple Health. If you grant permission, PickleStrong can read pickleball workouts, sleep, and heart-rate data to improve readiness context.

Does it sell my data?

No. PickleStrong is built around personal fitness tracking and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Privacy

Your body data stays personal.

PickleStrong stores profile choices, soreness entries, play sessions, routine completions, readiness context, and settings to provide app features. Apple Health access is optional and used only for features you choose, such as sleep, heart-rate, and pickleball workout context.
