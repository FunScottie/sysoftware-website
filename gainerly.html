Read-only by Design

Gainerly is not a wallet and cannot move funds.

Gainerly reads public blockchain data. It never asks for a seed phrase or private key, never signs transactions, and never has custody of crypto assets.

  • No wallet connection required
  • No transaction signing
  • No custody or private key access
  • Public wallet data only
FAQ
The important trust questions.

Is Gainerly a wallet?

No. Gainerly is a read-only portfolio and PnL tracker. It does not custody funds or sign transactions.

Do I need to connect Phantom?

No. Paste a public Solana wallet address or scan a wallet QR code. No wallet connection is required.

Is this financial advice?

No. Wallet intelligence is research based on public wallet history. Always verify data and make your own decisions.

Privacy

Public wallet data only. No custody. No private keys.

Gainerly uses public on-chain data and optional third-party data providers to build wallet summaries, PnL estimates, NFT views, and research signals. The app does not ask for seed phrases or private keys and cannot move funds.
