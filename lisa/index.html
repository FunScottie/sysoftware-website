Lisa Founder & personal concierge

Clear, respectful boundaries

Personal assistance — not caregiving.

Here With Lisa provides non-medical concierge and companion services. Lisa does not provide nursing, medication management, bathing, lifting, medical advice, or emergency care.

Financial and account-related help is limited to organization and assistance under clear written authorization. Lisa does not retain client PINs, passwords, or unrestricted access to funds.

Good questions

A few things families often want to know.

Every family and every season of life is different. The first conversation is simply a chance to listen, understand, and see whether Lisa is the right fit.

Is this home care or a medical service?+

No. Lisa provides non-medical personal assistance, companionship, errands, transportation, organization, and technology help. She does not provide personal care, nursing, or medical services.

Can family members receive updates?+

Yes. With the client's clear permission, Lisa can share practical updates after visits, appointments, or errands. The client's privacy and preferences always come first.

Can visits be recurring?+

Yes. Support can be arranged as an occasional visit, a focused project, or a dependable weekly routine. Recurring visits are a wonderful way to build familiarity and consistency.

How are services and pricing arranged?+

After a complimentary introduction, Lisa will recommend a simple hourly or recurring plan based on the type of assistance, visit length, and travel involved. Everything is agreed upon in advance.

What happens in an emergency?+

Here With Lisa is not an emergency service. Emergencies should always be directed to 911 or the appropriate medical provider. Client plans will include clear family and emergency contact information.
