A simple, satisfying puzzle

Pick a pug. Find its place. Reveal the scene.

Each scene starts as one big silhouette. Choose from the Pug Parade below, then place every character exactly where it belongs. There is no clock to beat—just a cheerful puzzle waiting to click into place.

1

See the silhouette

Look closely at the complete shape and notice the spaces each pug is meant to fill.

2

Choose from the parade

Browse a cast of costumed, playful pugs and pick the one you want to place next.

3

Complete the picture

Each perfect match reveals more of the scene, then unlocks a new storybook puzzle.

Puzzle at your pace

Easy to begin. Lovely to finish.

Every correct placement locks into the scene with a little burst of joy. Work in any order, take your time, and enjoy the finished picture before moving on.

  • Relaxed silhouette-matching gameplay
  • Handcrafted scenes that grow in complexity
  • Progress that saves right where you leave off
A growing storybook
