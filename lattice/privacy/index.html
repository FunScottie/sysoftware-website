Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026

Lattice has no ads and no third-party advertising trackers. Your gameplay data is stored on your device.

Who we are

Lattice is published by SY Software, an independent app studio founded by Scott Yale. For questions, contact support@sysoftware.com.

Information we collect

Lattice is designed to work without collecting personal information directly from you. The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Gameplay progress, daily puzzle results, scores, stars, streaks, and run history
  • Game settings, preferences, input mode choices, and tutorial state
  • Lattice Pro purchase status, as provided by Apple's purchase system

Apple may provide crash logs or diagnostics to developers if you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in iOS settings.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center features, Apple may process your Game Center profile, leaderboard scores, achievements, and related account information according to Apple's privacy practices. Lattice uses Game Center only to support leaderboards and related game features.

Purchases

Lattice Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. SY Software does not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase history and refund handling are managed by Apple.

How we use information

  • To save your game progress, results, settings, and Lattice Pro entitlement
  • To show your scores and achievements through Game Center if you use those features
  • To improve app quality using crash reports or diagnostics shared through Apple

Sharing

Lattice does not sell, rent, or share your personal data. The app does not include third-party advertising libraries. Game Center and purchases are handled by Apple when you use those features.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes the local data associated with Lattice. Game Center records and App Store purchase history are managed by Apple and may remain associated with your Apple account according to Apple's policies.

Children

Lattice is a puzzle game intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since Lattice stores gameplay data locally on your device, you can remove that data by deleting the app. For Apple-managed Game Center or purchase data, please use Apple's account and privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. If significant changes are made, the effective date at the top will be updated.
