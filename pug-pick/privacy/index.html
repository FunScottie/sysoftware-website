Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Pug Pick does not require an account, does not collect, sell, or share personal data, and does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Your game progress and preferences stay on your device.

Who we are

Pug Pick is published by SY Software, an independent app studio founded by Scott Yale. For privacy questions, contact support@sysoftware.com.

Information we collect

Pug Pick is designed to be enjoyed without creating an account or providing personal information. The current version does not collect personal data, use third-party analytics, or include third-party advertising trackers.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game, Pug Pick stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Puzzle progress and completed-level history
  • Your Pug Album collection
  • Preferences such as music, sound effects, haptics, and onboarding state

This information is used only to provide the game experience. SY Software does not operate a server for storing this data.

Network access

Pug Pick does not require an internet connection for normal play.

Apple services and diagnostics

Apple may provide crash logs or diagnostics to developers if you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings. Apple may also manage App Store distribution and device backups according to your Apple account and device settings.

Sharing

SY Software does not sell, rent, or share personal information. Pug Pick does not include third-party advertising libraries or behavioral tracking SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting Pug Pick generally deletes the app data stored locally with it. Device backups, if enabled, are managed according to your Apple settings.

Children

Pug Pick is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Pug Pick changes how it handles data. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
