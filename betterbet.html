Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

BetterBet is a personal casino session tracker. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data.

Effective date: April 1, 2026

Who we are

BetterBet is published by SY Software, an independent app studio founded by Scott Yale. For questions, contact support@sysoftware.com.

Information we collect

  • Session data you enter locally on your device — casino locations, game names, win/loss amounts, notes
  • Diagnostics automatically collected by Apple (crash logs), if you have sharing enabled in iOS settings
  • Anonymous, aggregated gameplay signals (e.g. game heat scores) if you opt in to Community Insights — no dollar amounts, session details, or personally identifiable information is ever transmitted

App permissions

  • Camera and Photo Library — only when you attach photos to sessions or games
  • Notifications — only if you enable session reminders in Settings

No other device permissions are required.

Sharing

BetterBet does not sell, rent, or share your personal data. No third-party advertising libraries are used in this app.

Data retention & deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes all associated data. You may also remove individual sessions or games at any time within the app.
