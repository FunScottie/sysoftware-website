FlareCare Tracker is a health companion for managing chronic pain and flare-ups. Log daily symptoms, identify triggers, visualize trends, and generate doctor-ready reports to share with your care team.

Built for clarity.

Every feature is designed to help you understand your pain patterns and explain them clearly to your healthcare team.

🗺️

Body Map Logging

Tap any joint to instantly rate pain levels. Quickly log the quality of pain (sharp, aching, burning), plus energy, mood, stress, and sleep in seconds.

📊

Trend Visualization

View charts showing pain patterns across days and weeks. Toggle between overall pain and specific body parts to spot what triggers your flares.

📈

Symptom Tracking

Log comprehensive symptom data: pain location and intensity, triggers, medications, energy levels, mood, stress, and sleep quality all in one place.

📄

Doctor-Ready Reports

Export PDF reports of your symptoms and patterns in seconds. Give your care team the data-backed information they need to make better treatment decisions.

🎯

Condition Profiles

Personalized setup for rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, fibromyalgia, and other conditions. Pre-selected body parts and tracking recommendations tailored to your needs.

🔒

Privacy First

Your health data stays on your device. No ads, no trackers, no third-party data sharing. Complete control over your personal health information.
