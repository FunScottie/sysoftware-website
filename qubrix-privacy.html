Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026

Qubrix is a 3D block puzzle game. Your gameplay progress and settings stay on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data.

Who we are

Qubrix is published by SY Software, an independent app studio founded by Scott Yale. For questions, contact support@sysoftware.com.

Information we collect

  • Gameplay progress, scores, streaks, settings, and purchase status stored locally on your device
  • Game Center scores and leaderboard data if you choose to use Apple Game Center
  • Diagnostics automatically collected by Apple, such as crash logs, if you have sharing enabled in iOS settings

App permissions

Qubrix does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library. Game Center features are handled by Apple when you sign in through iOS.

How we use information

  • Local gameplay data is used to save your progress, stats, settings, and high scores
  • Game Center data is used only for Apple-provided leaderboards and score submission
  • Diagnostic information, if shared with Apple, may be used to improve app stability and fix issues

In-app purchases

Qubrix Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. SY Software does not receive your payment card details. Purchase entitlement status may be stored on your device so the app can unlock Pro features.

Sharing

Qubrix does not sell, rent, or share your personal data. No third-party advertising libraries are used in this app.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes the local Qubrix data stored with it. Game Center leaderboard data is managed by Apple and can be controlled through your Apple account and iOS settings.

Children

Qubrix is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Your rights

If you are located in California, the EEA, or the UK, you may have additional rights such as access, correction, or deletion of your data. Since Qubrix stores gameplay data locally, you can exercise these rights by deleting the app or clearing data available in the app. For questions, contact support@sysoftware.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. If significant changes are made, the effective date at the top will be updated.
