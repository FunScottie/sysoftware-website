How It Plays

Tap a group. Confirm the clear. Let gravity settle the cube.

Any connected group of two or more same-color blocks can be removed. The bigger the group, the harder the score swings in your favor.

  • Tap once to preview a connected group
  • Tap again or confirm to remove it
  • Blocks fall into the empty spaces after each clear
  • Orbit the cube to read every face before committing
Score Targets

Build chains before valid moves disappear.

Each puzzle asks you to reach a target score. Clearing tiny groups keeps the board moving, but big groups, multipliers, and power blocks are where Qubrix opens up.

  • Bigger chains score exponentially more
  • Multipliers apply to the whole cleared group
  • Special blocks activate when included in a clear
  • Run out of valid moves and the attempt ends

Special Blocks
Power blocks turn matches into plans.
Bombs, rockets, magic cubes, and multipliers are mixed into the board. Include them in a cleared group to trigger their effects and open bigger routes through the cube.
BBomb

Blast nearby blocks.

Explodes in a radius and clears surrounding blocks when its group is removed.

RRocket

Sweep a row or column.

Fires across the board, clearing everything in its path.

MMagic

Bridge disconnected groups.

Acts as a wildcard, matching any color to create larger chains.

x3Multiplier

Scale the whole chain.

Doubles or triples the score for the group that includes it.
